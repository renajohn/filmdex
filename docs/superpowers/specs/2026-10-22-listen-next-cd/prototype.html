Listen Next — Digital | CD

Prototype with the real covers from prod. Digital: the current 10 albums of Listen Next. CD: the 23 CDs come in since September, the classical ones left out. Everything here is pretend: nothing is saved.

Laptop: the buttons show under the pointer

iPhone: always shown

What to try

  • Digital | CD next to the title switches lists; the band turns silver for CD, gold for Digital.
  • At the foot, the other list in small: a click on a cover shows it in its list (the band switches, scrolls to it and lights it a moment); to move it, drag it up. The label (“Digital · 10”) switches lists.
  • To move a cover to the other list, four ways to compare (top of the page): A, a round button with the other list's icon (the headphones in CD, the disc in Digital); B, in words, “→ Stream” or “→ CD”; C, a ⋯ menu with Move to and Take it off; D, no button at all.
  • Drag and drop, both ways: drag a cover onto the strip at the foot, or onto the other list's name in Digital | CD; drag a small cover of the foot up onto the row, or onto its list's name. With a mouse it drags at once; on the iPhone, hold the cover a moment (a quick swipe still scrolls the row).
  • In CD: no ▶ anywhere. ✕ takes a CD off once played; when it has no stars, a bubble asks for them for 7 s (Undo there too).
  • In Digital: ▶ plays (here, it only says so), Repeat as today.
  • “Simulate: a new CD is added”: it arrives first in CD, with a NEW tag.
  • The grid (twelve older CDs): its round button wears the list the album is in: gold headphones in Digital, a gold disc in CD. A click puts it in Digital, or takes it off its list; Alt-click on the Mac, or holding it on the iPhone (a ring grows), puts it in CD.

Not decided by this prototype

  • Which list a CD bought from the wish list lands in: CD, like any CD come in.
  • The ones already in Digital today (Franz Ferdinand, Moby, Queen…) stay there: moved like any other.
