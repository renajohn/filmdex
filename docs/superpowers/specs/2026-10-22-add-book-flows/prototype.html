Add a book — four flows

Search or photo, found or not. What is new is marked NEW.

What changes
  • A photo of the back that finds one edition opens it at once, marked found by its barcode: no list to pick from.
  • Not found is a step of a flow, not a dead end: a page with steps (back ✓, cover, read, review), its next step a big gold button. After a photo, the next step is the cover, asked at once.
  • After a typed search not found, the same steps start from the cover; typing it all by hand stays, smaller.
  • On a laptop the camera is a drop zone (or a file), the same steps beside each other.
